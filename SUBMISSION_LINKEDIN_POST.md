🚀 Excited to unveil AegisCare AI, built during the PromptWars × Catalyst Solo Hackathon 2026 powered by Google for Developers, Hack2Skill, and Makers Need More (MnM)!

📌 The Challenge: Elderly Care & Medication Management Platform
Managing polypharmacy is a major hurdle for elderly individuals. Complex dosages, memory lapses, and illegible prescriptions lead to preventable hospitalizations, while family members and caregivers endure constant anxiety over missed doses.

💡 What I Built:
AegisCare AI is a smart, interconnected healthcare ecosystem that bridges the gap between elderly seniors and their remote care network through passcode-gated access:
• 👵 Senior Mode (PIN: 1234): Designed specifically for elderly seniors with ultra-large typography, text-to-speech voice dose readouts, and massive 1-tap "✓ I TOOK THIS" confirmation buttons.
• 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Caregiver Portal (PIN: 5678): Provides real-time adherence analytics (94% compliance tracking), remote schedule editing, and proactive escalation alerts.

✨ What Makes AegisCare AI Distinctive:
1. 🧠 Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision OCR: Snapping a doctor's prescription automatically parses handwritten clinical notes and Latin sig codes (OD, BD, PC) into structured schedules with zero manual typing.
2. 🛡️ Clinical Drug-Drug & Food Interaction Guardian: Continuous AI monitoring detects harmful drug collisions and dietary contraindications (e.g. Statin + Grapefruit risks).
3. 🚨 Proactive Multi-Channel Escalation: If a dose remains unconfirmed for 60 minutes, the serverless notification engine automatically dispatches urgent email alerts to the family network.
4. 📄 Printable Doctor Visit Summary: Generates a clinical consultation sheet with full adherence telemetry and active regimens.

🛠️ Technology Stack:
• Frontend: Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion
• AI Engine: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API (@google/genai SDK)
• Architecture: Serverless Edge API Routes, Zod Schema Validation
• DevOps: GitHub Actions CI/CD, Multi-Stage Dockerfile, Single-Branch Public Repository (<1 MB)
