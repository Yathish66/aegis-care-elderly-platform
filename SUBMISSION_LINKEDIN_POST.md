👵 The Problem:
Managing polypharmacy is one of the most perilous challenges confronting aging seniors. Small, illegible pill labels, complex multi-dose schedules, and cognitive memory lapses lead to millions of preventable hospitalizations annually. Concurrently, remote adult children and family caregivers suffer from chronic anxiety over missed doses.

💡 The Solution — AegisCare AI:
AegisCare bridges the generational divide with a zero-friction, passcode-protected dual-interface ecosystem:
• 👵 Senior Mode (PIN: 1234): Tailored for elderly ergonomics with ultra-large typography, high-contrast vision modes (WCAG 2.1 AAA), natural text-to-speech audio dose readouts, and massive 1-tap "✓ I TOOK THIS" confirmation buttons.
• 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Caregiver Portal (PIN: 5678): Empowers remote families with real-time adherence analytics (94% compliance tracking), vitals telemetry correlation, doorstep pharmacy auto-refills, and automated escalation dispatch.

⭐ Key Architectural Innovations & Star-Synthesis:
1. 🧠 Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision OCR: Snaps physical prescription slips and instantly parses handwritten clinical notes and Latin sig codes (OD, BD, PC) into safe medication schedules.
2. 💊 Multimodal AI Pill Identifier: Visual inspection matching loose tablets by shape, imprint, and color bands against active prescriptions to prevent fatal drug mix-ups.
3. 📦 Virtual Medibox™ 7-Day IoT Dispenser: Synthesized from top open-source smart hardware architectures, featuring active pulsing LED compartment alerts (Morning, Noon, Evening, Bedtime) and 1-click dispensing.
4. 🪪 Clinical ER Medical Passport: 1-Click printable paramedic wallet card and FHIR JSON export with verified FDA NDC codes, blood group (B+), and critical allergy warnings.

6. 🔐 Zero-Knowledge HIPAA & PHI Data Sovereignty: Local-first encrypted client storage, tamper-evident audit logging, and 5-attempt brute-force lockout defense.
